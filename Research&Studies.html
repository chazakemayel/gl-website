Research & Studies

Turning Beirut air-quality data into public knowledge.

Green Leaders is measuring air quality in Beirut, conducting research, observing trends, and translating findings into accessible reports that support awareness and environmental action.

Read Publications View Live Data
Beirut air-quality study

From field measurements to evidence-based environmental communication.

The research initiative focuses on monitoring air pollutants in Beirut, comparing changes over time, identifying meaningful patterns, and publishing findings in a format that communities, students, institutions, and decision-makers can understand.

BeirutStudy Area
LiveMonitoring

Research focus

  • Measure air quality indicators through monitoring tools.
  • Study trends and variations across the observation period.
  • Share initial findings through English and Arabic reports.
  • Support public awareness through accessible environmental data.
Research workflow

How the study is built.

The study connects data collection, interpretation, and communication so research becomes understandable and useful.

01

Measure

Collect air-quality readings and monitor pollutants through the Beirut monitoring setup.

02

Analyze

Review changes, compare indicators, and identify the most relevant environmental patterns.

03

Interpret

Translate measurements into clear observations that can be understood by non-specialists.

04

Publish

Share findings through reports, newsletters, education material, and public communication.

Initial findings

Early insights from Beirut’s air-quality study.

The initial findings summarize the first observations from the monitoring work and create a baseline for future research and public awareness.

Pollutant visibility

The study helps make invisible air pollutants more visible by presenting readings and trends in a clear, accessible way.

Urban context

Findings are interpreted within Beirut’s dense urban environment, where transport, energy, and local activity affect air quality.

Public communication

Reports in English and Arabic help reach a wider audience and support informed environmental discussions.
