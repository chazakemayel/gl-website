How it works

A guided audit process from observation to measurable savings.

The platform turns simple inputs into a structured assessment, helping teams move from scattered notes to a clear picture of consumption, leaks, and potential savings.

1

Collect Inputs

Enter basic facility information, occupancy, audited rooms, fixture types, and operating patterns to establish a useful water baseline.

2

Analyze & Benchmark

Review outputs through organized indicators and charts that make consumption patterns easier to understand and compare.

3

Save Water

Document leak areas, propose saving measures, and communicate practical recommendations through a professional report.
