How It Works

A simple step-by-step process to help schools and institutions understand consumption, identify leaks, and improve efficiency.

1) Collect Inputs

Enter basic information about your facility and water usage to establish a baseline.

2) Analyze & Benchmark

Review usage patterns and compare performance across different areas and periods.

3) Save Water

Get actionable recommendations to reduce consumption, costs, and environmental impact.
