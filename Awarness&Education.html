Awareness & Education

Our education and awareness initiatives empower students, educators, and communities to adopt sustainable practices — from energy efficiency and water conservation to air quality and circular economy.

Sustainability Movement Campaign

Building Environmental Stewardship Through Schools

Sustainability Movement, the education and awareness program of Green Leaders NGO, has made a profound impact on students, teachers, and communities. This campaign is the result of collaboration with the Center for Green Schools at USGBC and partnerships with corporates (DAR Design Offices) and MENA Green Building Councils.

Together, they have strengthened the program, engaging over 1,200 teachers and 90,000 students in hands-on Green Apple Day of Service projects, transforming learning environments and fostering environmental stewardship.

1,200+ Teachers engaged through trainings and school initiatives
90,000+ Students reached through hands-on sustainability projects
Partnership-driven USGBC Center for Green Schools + corporates + MENA GBCs
What Participants Do

Hands-On Activities & Learning

Participants engage in practical sustainability solutions, including energy utilization and passive design, solar passive cooling experiments, water audits, classroom air quality monitoring, and recycling & upcycling activities. The program also emphasizes healthy classrooms—where indoor air quality, thermal comfort, and daylight access support student well-being and performance.

Energy & Passive Design

Hands-on experiments exploring efficient energy use, passive strategies, and solar cooling principles.

Water Audits & Conservation

School-based audits and practical steps to reduce consumption, improve efficiency, and protect water quality.

Air Quality Monitoring

Classroom monitoring that builds awareness around indoor air quality and its impact on health and learning.

Recycling & Upcycling

Interactive waste reduction activities that encourage circular thinking and responsible resource use.

Teacher Training

Training programs that equip educators with sustainability foundations to inspire future generations.

Green Champion Challenge

Student teams compete to design innovative sustainability solutions—building leadership and teamwork.
