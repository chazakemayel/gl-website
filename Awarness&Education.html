Awareness & Education

Turning classrooms into living sustainability labs.

Green Leaders empowers students, teachers, and communities to understand sustainability through action: energy, water, air quality, circular economy, and healthy learning environments.

Explore Mood Boards Join the Movement
Sustainability Movement Campaign

Building environmental stewardship through schools.

Sustainability Movement is the education and awareness program of Green Leaders NGO. It has made a strong impact on students, teachers, and communities by translating environmental concepts into practical school activities.

The campaign was strengthened through collaboration with the Center for Green Schools at USGBC and partnerships with corporates such as DAR Design Offices and MENA Green Building Councils.

1,200+ Teachers engaged through trainings and school initiatives.
90,000+ Students reached through hands-on Green Apple Day of Service projects.
Partners USGBC Center for Green Schools, corporates, and MENA Green Building Councils.
What Participants Do

Hands-on learning that students can see, measure, and improve.

The program connects sustainability concepts to real school spaces through experiments, audits, monitoring activities, and student-led challenges.

Energy & Passive Design

Experiments exploring efficient energy use, passive design strategies, and solar passive cooling principles.

Water Audits & Conservation

School-based water audits that identify savings opportunities and promote responsible consumption.

Air Quality Monitoring

Classroom monitoring that connects indoor air quality with health, attention, and learning conditions.

Recycling & Upcycling

Creative circular economy activities that reduce waste and encourage responsible material use.

Teacher Training

Training sessions that equip educators with tools to bring sustainability into daily learning.

Green Champion Challenge

Student teams design practical sustainability solutions while building leadership and teamwork.

Learning Journey

From awareness to measurable action.

Each activity is designed to move participants from observation to experimentation, implementation, and communication.

1

Observe

Students identify sustainability issues in their school environment.

2

Measure

Teams collect simple data on water, air, energy, or waste patterns.

3

Act

They test practical interventions and behavior-change actions.

4

Share

Projects are communicated through campaigns, mood boards, and school events.
