Who We Are

Empowering sustainability through education, research, and collective action.

Founded in Lebanon in 2023, Green Leaders is a non-governmental organization committed to advancing sustainable development and environmental stewardship. While primarily operating in Lebanon, our reach extends across the MENA region, with global partnerships amplifying our impact.

Our mission is to foster environmental awareness, drive sustainability research, and implement transformative initiatives in energy efficiency, water stewardship, pollution mitigation, and climate resilience. Through data-driven strategies and collaborative partnerships, we address critical environmental challenges and contribute to global sustainability goals.

As of January 2025, Green Leaders brings together 30 dedicated professionals and student members, actively working to advance the organization’s mission and accelerate efforts to combat climate change.

Advocating for circular economy principles and responsible resource management, we strive to build a regenerative and resilient future. Our approach is built on collaboration. By forging strategic partnerships with governments, businesses, academic institutions, and local communities, Green Leaders bridges diverse sectors to deliver scalable, impactful solutions.
